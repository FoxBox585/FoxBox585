## 🧪 The Lab

### 🌐 [OpenSchwa](https://github.com/openschwa) — *because [ə] belongs to everyone*

[Schwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwa) is the most common vowel sound in
English — the humble *uh* in **a·bout**. It's also the name of my main project:
**open-source phonetics practice**, free for learners, teachers and anyone who
has ever argued about a vowel.

🐙 Code: [`github.com/openschwa`](https://github.com/openschwa) &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🌍 Site: [openschwa.ru](https://openschwa.ru)
